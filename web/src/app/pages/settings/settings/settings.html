Rules

  • A token without a value (no release group, no year…) becomes empty, and brackets left empty by it, [] or (), are removed.
  • : becomes -; other characters not allowed in file names (/ \ * ? " < > |) are dropped. Repeated spaces collapse and trailing dots are trimmed.
  • File formats get the original file extension appended; don't add one yourself.
  • A file holding several episodes lists them as E01-E02 in {{ '{Episode}' }} and joins their titles with +.
  • {{ '{Series Year}' }} is left empty when the series title already ends with a year, e.g. "Doctor Who (2005)".
  • Season folders are only used for series that have "Use season folders" enabled.
  • Formats apply to new imports. To bring existing series in line, use Organize on the series page; it shows a preview first.
