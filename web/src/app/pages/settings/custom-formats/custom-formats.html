Custom formats

Named sets of conditions (HDR, audio, codec, edition, streaming service, release group…). Each quality profile gives the formats a score: releases are ranked by quality, then by score, and a higher score counts as an upgrade until the profile's "upgrade until" score. Paste formats from the TRaSH Guides, Radarr or Sonarr with Import.
